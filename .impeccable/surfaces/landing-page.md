## Scope

Landing page — the public-facing page that explains KnowThyNeighbor and gets couples to sign up.

## Visitor mode

Persuade

## Audience

Any couple feeling disconnected from their neighborhood. Both active seekers and curious skeptics. The page must make the concept immediately clear and lower the barrier.

## Job

Understand what KnowThyNeighbor does, believe it's safe and worthwhile, and sign up.

## Constraints

- No tech/startup vibes
- No corporate polish
- No cutesy/whimsical
- "Old school as possible" (user-pinned)
- All content authored synthetic — no real testimonials, photos, or press
- No street address collected — only zip code

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a cork bulletin board in a neighborhood coffee shop — pinned cards, flyers with tear-off tabs, pushpin accents, blue-ink handwriting. The category-default arrangement (hero stock photo + 3-column feature grid + testimonials) is refused in favor of a physical analog surface you browse like a real board.

OWN-WORLD: Cork brown ground (#C4A366) occupying the full surface. Cream paper cards (#FDF8ED) with subtle curl and shadow. Red pushpin (#CC4433) for primary accents and CTAs. Blue ink (#2B4570) for body text and handwriting. Green thumbtack (#5B7F5E) as secondary accent. Paper has torn edges and slight yellowing. Cards are slightly rotated and overlapping like a real board. Pushpins cast small shadows. No gradients, no glass, no tech chrome.

STORY: The visitor lands on a familiar surface — a bulletin board they've seen in every coffee shop, library, and community center. They immediately understand: this is a neighborhood thing, not a tech product. The main flyer explains KnowThyNeighbor in hand-lettered style. Surrounding cards show what it looks like in practice — a couple's profile card, a dinner invite, availability notes. The visitor tears a tab (clicks sign up) because the board made the concept feel ordinary, safe, and neighborly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The full viewport is the cork board — not header-on-cork, actual full-bleed cork texture with subtle grain. Dead center, a large cream paper flyer pinned with a red pushpin, rotated 1-2 degrees: "KnowThyNeighbor" in bold condensed marker-style lettering at the top, then "Find couples in your neighborhood for shared meals" in blue-ink handwriting below. At the bottom of the flyer: a row of tear-off tabs with "Sign Up" repeated, one tab visually half-torn as the affordance. Flanking the main flyer at varying pin-angles: a couple profile card ("The Patels · 2.3 mi · Hosts"), a handwritten note on a yellow index card ("Looking for dinner friends — Saturdays work best!"), a small calendar card with checked availability slots. The primary action IS the tear-off tab — not a floating button.

FORM: The Bulletin Board, candidate #1 resonance ranking, seed key fe89b371. Signature interaction: hovering a card lifts it slightly and brightens its shadow as if you're pulling it toward you; tearing a sign-up tab animates the paper separating. Scrolling down reveals more of the board with additional pinned content sections.
